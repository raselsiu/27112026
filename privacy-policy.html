2. Information We Do Not Directly Collect & Local Storage

No account required — study data stays on your device

The App does not require an account, sign-in or registration. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number or password to use its core learning features. The developer does not operate an account system or upload your study history to a developer-controlled cloud server.

The following activity and preferences are designed to be stored locally on your device using Android local storage, such as SharedPreferences and app-private storage:

Quiz activityQuiz progress, answers and exam result history
Saved questionsBookmarked and favourite MCQs
Learning toolsDaily Challenge and Smart Revision activity
App preferencesTheme and other app settings
Generated documentsPDF files created by the App and stored on your device
PDF sharingWhen you share a PDF, the selected destination app handles it under its own policies
Local storage: These items are not intentionally transmitted to a developer-operated server. They remain in app-local storage unless you choose to share or export them.
Important distinction: Third-party SDKs, including Google AdMob, may independently collect or process certain device and advertising data. “No account” and “local study data” do not mean that no data is processed by any third party.

3. Google AdMob & Third-Party Advertising

Advertising services used to support the free App

The App is offered free of charge and may display Banner, Interstitial and Rewarded advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob).

To serve ads, measure performance, manage ad frequency, detect invalid activity and prevent advertising fraud, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Android Advertising ID or other available advertising identifiers;
  • IP address, which may be used to infer a general or approximate location;
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system, language and technical identifiers;
  • Ad impressions, clicks, interactions and information about ad delivery;
  • Diagnostics, performance and app interaction information relevant to advertising services.

The exact data processed may depend on your device, Android version, consent choices, Google services, SDK version and the advertising configuration used in the App. This information is handled under Google’s own privacy terms and practices.

4. App Permissions & External Links

Network access and links opened outside the App

Based on the app configuration described by the developer, the App uses these network permissions:

INTERNET ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

  • INTERNET: Allows the App and supported SDK services to access the internet, including loading advertisements and opening a Google search request when you choose that feature.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the App or SDK to check network connectivity status.

The App does not intentionally request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions for its core features. PDF sharing may use Android’s secure FileProvider mechanism rather than broad storage access.

Permission verification: Google Mobile Ads SDK versions or build configurations may add permissions (for example, AD_ID) through Android manifest merging. Before publishing, check the final merged/release manifest in Android Studio and ensure this section matches the permissions actually included in your APK/AAB.

Search on Google: If you select this option from a question menu, the App opens a Google Search page or query in an external browser or supported app. Information processed after leaving the App is subject to Google’s and the browser provider’s privacy policies.

5. Data Deletion & User Control

You remain in control of locally stored learning data

You can manage or remove local app data using the data-management options available in the App:

  • Open Settings → Data Management (if available in your installed version).
  • Choose the option to clear quiz progress, result history, saved questions or all local app data.
  • You can also uninstall the App to remove its app-private local data. Files exported or saved outside the App may need to be deleted separately.

Advertising-related data handled by Google is separate from the App’s local study data. You can manage available advertising controls through your Android or Google account settings, subject to Google’s current options and retention practices.

No developer account deletion request is needed for locally stored study data because the App does not provide user accounts or store that study history on a developer-operated server.

6. Children’s Privacy

Age-appropriate use and third-party services

ICT MCQ Master is primarily designed for learners and candidates preparing for BCS, NTRCA, Bank, PSC, Primary and other ICT-related examinations. The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

Advertising configuration, age treatment and consent requirements must follow applicable Google Play and Google AdMob policies and the laws of the regions where the App is distributed.
