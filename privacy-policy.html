Welcome to Our Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how বর্ণমালা ও সংখ্যা পরিচয় handles information when you use our educational mobile application. Please read this policy carefully to understand our privacy practices.

01 Introduction

Welcome to “বর্ণমালা ও সংখ্যা পরিচয়” (“the App”).

The App is an educational mobile application designed primarily for children to learn Bengali letters, numbers, basic concepts, and other educational content in a simple, safe, and enjoyable way.

We respect the privacy of children, parents, guardians, and all users. Protecting children's privacy is an important part of how we design and operate this App.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the App, how third-party advertising services work, and what privacy choices may be available to users and parents.

Our Commitment: The developer does not directly collect or maintain a database of users' personal information through the App's educational features. However, certain third-party services, particularly Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), may process limited technical and advertising-related information as described below.

By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires parental consent or another legal basis, this statement does not replace that requirement.

02 Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Do Not Directly Collect

The App does not require users to register, create an account, or provide personal information to access its main educational features.

We do not directly ask children or other users to provide:

  • Full name
  • Home or postal address
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Passwords or account credentials
  • Contacts or contact lists
  • Photos or videos
  • Precise GPS location
  • Financial or payment information
  • Government identification numbers
We do not operate a user registration system or a personal-information database for the App's educational functionality.

2.2 Information That May Be Processed by Third-Party Services

Although we do not directly collect personal information from children, the App integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display advertisements.

When advertisements are requested, displayed, or measured, Google and its services may automatically process certain technical and advertising-related information, depending on the user's device, settings, region, and applicable advertising policies.

This information may include:

  • Device information and technical characteristics
  • App information and version
  • IP address
  • Device or other IDs, including advertising-related identifiers where permitted
  • Advertising request and interaction information
  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address
  • Diagnostic, performance, fraud-prevention, and security information

Purpose of processing: Device or other IDs may be used by Google AdMob for advertising and marketing-related purposes, including delivering advertisements, measuring advertising performance, and providing related advertising services, subject to applicable child-directed advertising restrictions and user settings.

These activities are performed by the relevant third-party service under its own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

We do not independently collect or store this advertising-related information in our own user database. However, we cannot claim that no information is processed by third-party SDKs.

03 Children's Privacy and COPPA

The App is primarily designed for children and families, including children under the age of 13.

We take children's privacy seriously and aim to follow applicable children's privacy laws and platform requirements, including:

  • The United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the COPPA Rule.
  • Google Play Families Policy.
  • Applicable child-directed advertising requirements.
  • Other applicable children's privacy and data-protection laws.

Our Children's Privacy Practices

  • We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information.
  • We do not require children to create accounts.
  • We do not provide user profiles or social networking features.
  • We do not knowingly collect children's precise location.
  • We do not intentionally use children's information for behavioral advertising or remarketing.
  • We configure Google Mobile Ads requests for child-directed treatment, as applicable.
  • We aim to ensure that advertisements shown to children are appropriate for their intended age group.

Parental Rights

Parents and legal guardians may contact us if they have questions or concerns about children's privacy or believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us.

Where applicable law gives parents rights to review, request deletion of, or object to the processing of a child's personal information, we will review such requests and take appropriate action as required by law.

Important: If a third-party service processes information through the App, its collection and processing practices are also governed by that service's privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

05 Child-Directed Advertising and Advertising Restrictions

Because the App is primarily intended for children, we take additional precautions regarding advertising.

Our Google Mobile Ads implementation is configured to apply child-directed advertising treatment, including:

  • Child age-restricted advertising treatment.
  • A maximum ad content rating of G (General Audiences).
  • Restrictions intended to prevent personalized advertising and remarketing for child-directed ad requests.
  • Advertising safeguards provided by Google's child-directed advertising systems.

We do not intentionally serve personalized or interest-based advertisements to children.

We also aim to follow Google Play Families advertising requirements, including requirements concerning age-appropriate ad content and advertising formats.

We do not guarantee that every advertisement will be suitable in every circumstance. Advertising content and availability may depend on Google's systems, advertiser inventory, regional requirements, and applicable policies. We encourage parents and guardians to contact us if they encounter an advertisement that appears inappropriate for children.

06 Google UMP and Privacy Choices
